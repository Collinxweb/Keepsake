Save your chats?

While recording is on, your messages are saved to your Keepsake memory. Nothing is recorded unless you turn it on, and you can pause, export or delete at any time.

Don't type passwords, keys or other secrets.

Connect your agent

Your key is encrypted and kept in a private cookie. It is only used to send your messages to the provider you choose, and it is never shown again.
