Connect your agent

Your key is encrypted and stored in a private cookie. It is only used to send your messages to the provider you pick, and it is never shown again.

Save this chat to memory?

While this is on, your messages are saved to your memory. Chats are never recorded unless you turn it on, and you can pause, export or delete at any time.

Don't type passwords, keys or other secrets. In this demo, nothing leaves your device.
